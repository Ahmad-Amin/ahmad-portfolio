FinScan AI is a cross-platform expense tracker built with React Native and
Expo, available on Google Play. Its core idea is that nobody keeps a budget up
to date by typing every purchase by hand, so the app captures spending from
two places people already have: a photo of a receipt, and the SMS alerts their
bank already sends.

## The problem

Expense apps tend to fail on the same step: entry. Logging each purchase
manually works for a week, and then the log goes stale and the budget stops
meaning anything. The goal was to make the default path require no typing at
all.

## Two ways to capture an expense without typing

**Snap a receipt.** Take a photo, or pick one from the gallery, and AI
extracts the merchant, amount, date and category. Every receipt is also saved
to a gallery for later reference.

**Read bank SMS in the background.** Many banks text a transaction alert for
every purchase. A native Kotlin broadcast receiver picks up incoming bank SMS
in the background and logs the transaction automatically, without the app
being opened. This is the fastest path, since the log stays accurate with no
effort at all.

## How parsing works

The parsing step goes through a small set of serverless functions on Vercel.
They forward the receipt or SMS content to the OpenAI API and return
structured data (merchant, amount, category) to the app.

## Features around the capture

- **Multi-wallet tracking.** Cash, cards and accounts are tracked separately,
  with income, expenses and balance for each wallet and a total across all of
  them, and no bank login needed.
- **Analytics.** Weekly and monthly spending with category breakdowns, a
  tappable spending calendar and PDF export.
- **Budgets.** A monthly budget with a notification as you approach the limit,
  before you overspend instead of after.
- **Multi-currency**, a biometric app lock, dark mode, and optional spending
  reminders and weekly summaries.

## Business model

The app is free to download. Premium is a single one-time purchase, handled
through RevenueCat, that unlocks unlimited receipt scans and faster AI
parsing, and removes the monthly limits. There is no subscription.

## Stack

The app is built with React Native, Expo and Expo Router in TypeScript, with
SQLite in the stack, a native Kotlin module for SMS, the OpenAI API for
parsing, RevenueCat for purchases, and Vercel for the serverless functions.
